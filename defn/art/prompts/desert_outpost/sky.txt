Create the sky layer of a modular tiling background set for DEFN, a contemporary-to-near-future military
belt-scroller. This image is not a scene. It is the backmost layer of a parallax stack: nothing but sky and
atmosphere, filling the frame edge to edge and top to bottom, with every other element of the desert drawn on
separate layers in front of it. It contains no ground, no horizon line, no landforms, and no silhouettes of any
kind.

No reference image is attached, deliberately. This layer shares no subject matter with any other DEFN asset,
and an attached background is reproduced rather than merely referenced: on an earlier set, a sky given a
ground plane as a palette reference drew that ground plane across its own bottom third.

Draw it as authored 2D digital cartoon illustration in the DEFN house style. Sky is the one place the style
permits soft gradients, so use them: this layer is a smooth vertical wash and nothing else. It carries no
clouds at all -- the clouds are a separate drifting layer in front of it, and a cloud drawn here would sit
still while those move.

Grade the wash vertically, and make it unmistakably the sky of a hot, dry, open desert afternoon. The top of
the frame is a deep, clean, slightly dusty cerulean, saturated enough to feel like high desert air. It
lightens and warms steadily downward, passing through a pale sun-bleached blue, then a soft dusty rose, and
settling into a broad warm cream-gold haze band across the bottom third -- the thickened, dust-carrying light
that sits low over an arid basin. The lowest fifth of the frame is the palest and least saturated part of the
image, close to a warm bone white, because distant mesas will be drawn against it and need to separate from
it.

Keep every transition between those bands long, smooth and continuous. There is no band edge, no visible
boundary, and no step anywhere in the wash; each colour arrives gradually out of the one above it.

Light the sky evenly across its width. There is no sun disc, no sun position, no bright patch, no glow, no god
rays, no heat shimmer, and no lateral brightness gradient of any kind: the left edge, the centre and the right
edge are equally bright and equally saturated. The only change in value or hue across this image runs from top
to bottom.

Make the image perfectly seamless from left to right for continuous horizontal scrolling. The value bands, the
haze height, and the hue at every height must be identical at the left and right boundaries. With nothing drawn
in it this is nearly automatic, but the wash must still not drift in brightness or temperature from one side to
the other.

Deliver only sky. The image is sky from the very top edge to the very bottom edge, with atmosphere as its only
subject. There is no ground, no sand, no dune, no mesa, no rock, no road, no horizon line, no structures, no
towers, no masts, no birds, no aircraft, no people, no clouds of any kind, and no lettering. The bottom edge of
the frame is open haze that other layers will be drawn over.

Avoid photorealism, photographic sky texture, realistic atmospheric scattering, volumetric light, painterly
concept-art brushwork, dramatic skies, storm clouds, sunsets, sun discs, lens flare, bloom, film grain, noisy
gradients, colour banding, clouds, a visible horizon, and visible tiling seams.
