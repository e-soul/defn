A 2D belt scroller video game background depicting a dense tropical jungle with ancient stone ruins, banana trees, and lush ferns.
Presentation Constraints:
Layout: Horizontal 16:9 composition, ultra-high resolution, crisp 4K fidelity graphics.
Perspective & Playable Area: Designed for a 2D belt scroller game. The bottom half (50%) of the entire screen must be a wide, flat dirt and grass ground plane (playable floor) seen from a high-angle, three-quarters top-down perspective. This large ground plane must provide massive vertical space for characters to walk up and down across the depth of the field.
Horizon Line: The horizon line and the edge of the jungle treeline must start exactly halfway up the screen (at the 50% vertical mark), keeping the entire bottom half open as a wide walking surface.
Tiling: Left-to-right horizontally seamless tiling background. The left edge must perfectly loop and match the right edge for continuous scrolling.
Artistic Style Architecture:
Art Style: High-quality, clean 2D vector art, sharp digital illustration, side-scroller cartoon asset style.
Outlines: Distinct, smooth, solid dark outlines tracking every leaf, vine, and stone structure, matching the line weight of Reference Images 1 and 2.
Shading & Texture: Smooth digital gradients, soft ambient shadows, and clean color blocks. No hand-painted brush textures, no sketchy lines, and no realistic grit.
Color Palette: Saturated jungle greens, deep teal shadows, and warm sunlight highlights filtering through the canopy, maintaining the clean digital tone of the provided references.
Reference Directives:
Extract the clean vector linework, object framing, and smooth gradient shading style from two reference character images.
Extract the environmental layering and atmospheric depth from the background reference image, but expand the road/path format to fill the entire lower 50% of the canvas.
