THE TWO SIDE MARGINS ARE THE MOST IMPORTANT INSTRUCTION IN THIS PROMPT. LAYOUT: the upper HALF of the canvas is pure flat magenta and holds no artwork. Nothing here is tall, dramatic, monumental or central: this is low clutter lying on a beach, seen from across the sand.
