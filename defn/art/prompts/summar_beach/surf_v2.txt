One isolated shoreline strip for DEFN, a hand-authored 2D cartoon military belt-scroller. Broad flat local-colour shapes, restrained values, gently irregular hand-drawn edges. This is a cut-out layer over a chroma backdrop, not a landscape, and it is a surface lying flat on the ground plane, so it carries NO INK LINES OF ANY KIND. Every boundary in it is one flat colour meeting another.

LAYOUT FIRST, BECAUSE IT IS ARITHMETIC. The canvas is an extremely wide, short strip. Measuring down from the top: the upper NINE TENTHS are pure flat chroma magenta RGB 255,0,255 with absolutely no artwork. All artwork is squeezed into the LAST TENTH of the height, along the bottom edge. This is not a composition preference: the game scales this layer to a small fraction of the screen, so a shallow drawn band becomes a long on-screen tile and a deep one repeats every few seconds. A band that fills half the canvas is a failed image, however good it looks.

Inside that shallow strip, only two things, one above the other. On top, a lace of clean off-white sea foam. Below it, dark wet sand, reaching down to the bottom edge of the canvas. The strip is fully opaque across the ENTIRE width, with no hole, gap or magenta inside it, and it touches the bottom edge everywhere.

The foam is the top boundary and the most varied edge in the image: rounded tongues of swash push up, a few thin fingers reach further than their neighbours, shallow scallops sit between them, and the height changes constantly along the width, never holding steady for long. A handful of small detached rounded foam blobs may float just above the lace. The foam is a single flat off-white shape with one faint cool blue-grey tone under it. No bubbles, no spray, no droplets, no texture, and no outline.

The wet sand below it is a dark cool amber-brown, clean and warm, with a faint teal sheen in the thinnest water just under the foam. At most one intermediate tone. Keep it quiet and low contrast; the foam is the only strong contrast in the image.

CRITICAL: there is no dark line, dark rim, drop shadow, edge stroke or outline anywhere in this image, and especially not where the artwork meets the magenta. The colour simply stops. An outlined edge here would show as a drawn rule across the game screen.

Light is flat across the width: no directional sun, sun glitter, lateral gradient, brighter middle, vignette or side shadow. Vertical value change is wanted. Every artwork boundary is a HARD CEL EDGE against the perfectly flat magenta field, crisp and fully opaque, with no feathering, glow, blur, haze, transparency or magenta mixed into the artwork. No pink, mauve, purple or magenta anywhere in the artwork.

Seamlessness is ONLY the two outer edges continuing each other: at the left-most 4% and right-most 4% the foam sits at exactly the same height with the same quiet profile, the same colours and the same values, interchangeable, and no distinctive tongue, finger or foam blob crosses either boundary. Never achieve the wrap by repeating the interior: no arrangement or silhouette occurs twice, and there is no even rhythm, row or grid of foam tongues.

No sea, water body, waves, horizon, sky, islands, dry sand, dunes, cliffs, rocks, cracked earth, stones, shells, driftwood, seaweed, footprints, characters, animals, birds, palms, cast shadows, text, letters, numerals, symbols, logos, frame or UI. No photographic texture, 3D rendering, fine repeated foam detail, grain or painterly brushwork.
