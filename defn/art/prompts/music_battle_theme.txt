Gradually add percussion layers, bass movement, counter-rhythms, and harmonic tension as if enemy pressure is increasing. Include a memorable but understated central motif and several rises and releases in intensity.

The track must remain clear and unobtrusive beneath gunfire, impacts, interface sounds, and repeated gameplay.
