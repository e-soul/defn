One original hand-inked 2D cartoon sea-and-islands stratum for DEFN, a contemporary military belt-scroller: crisp graphic silhouettes, simplified chunky geometry, broad flat local-colour regions, restrained two-value cel shading, gently irregular authored edges. This is an isolated cut-out layer, not a scene and not a repeating texture.

LAYOUT IS A TECHNICAL REQUIREMENT, because it sets the on-screen period of a scrolling layer: the upper HALF of the canvas is pure flat chroma magenta RGB 255,0,255 and holds no artwork whatsoever. All artwork lives in the LOWER HALF. Across the whole width, a dead level horizontal sea horizon runs at exactly one third of the way down that lower half, at the same height on the left edge as on the right. Below the horizon the water is an opaque continuous body of colour reaching the very bottom edge of the canvas. Above the horizon, small distant islands and low headlands rise as varied silhouettes against the magenta.

The islands are low, rounded, tropical and modest: gentle green-grey humps, one or two with a flat sandy shelf at the waterline, a few carrying tiny grouped palm crowns as small clustered shapes rather than drawn fronds. None is tall, dramatic, volcanic or central. Their tops form a strongly varied line with wide open stretches of bare horizon between groups, because this band is mostly seen between near beach objects and its top silhouette carries almost all of its value. Fine horizontal variation matters more than any single large form.

The water reads as tropical shallows deepening away from the viewer: a narrow strip of pale cool aqua just under the horizon, then bands of turquoise, then a wider quiet field of deeper cerulean-blue filling the bottom third of the artwork. Those bands are broad, softly wavering horizontal shapes of flat colour with no outline. The lower quarter of the water must be especially quiet, nearly a single even blue, with no whitecaps, no wave marks, no sparkle and nothing distinctive in it: a near band drawn later will slide across it and any feature there would visibly drift. Put the few small white cel-shaped ripple dashes only in the pale strip immediately under the horizon, sparse and unevenly spaced.

Distance means lighter, cooler values, lower contrast and thinner blue-grey contours on the islands, NEVER blur, haze, fog, fading or transparency. Keep the islands relatively light so warm golden sand and dark driftwood will separate from them later. Every artwork boundary is a HARD CEL EDGE against the entirely flat magenta backdrop, without glow, softness, feathering or colour contamination. Nothing in the artwork is pink, purple, mauve or magenta.

Lighting is uniform across the width: no directional sun, no sun glitter path, no lateral gradient, bright middle, vignette or side shadow. Vertical depth changes are wanted. Seamlessness is ONLY the two outer edges continuing each other: at the left-most 4% and right-most 4% there is open water and empty horizon at exactly the same heights, colours and values, interchangeable, with no island, headland or ripple crossing either boundary. Never achieve the wrap by repeating the interior; no island group, silhouette, arrangement or colour sequence occurs twice, and there are no rows, tiers or evenly spaced rhythm.

No beach, sand, foam, surf line, shore, boats, ships, piers, buoys, characters, animals, birds, sky wash, clouds, sun, foreground props, text, letters, numerals, glyphs, signs, logos, frame or UI. No photographic texture, 3D rendering, fine repeated wave detail, grain or painterly brushwork.
