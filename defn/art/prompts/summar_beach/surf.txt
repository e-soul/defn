One isolated shoreline band for DEFN, a contemporary military belt-scroller drawn as authored 2D cartoon illustration: broad flat local-colour shapes, restrained values, gently irregular hand-drawn edges. This is a cut-out layer, not a complete landscape. IMPORTANT EXCEPTION TO THE HOUSE STYLE: this is a surface lying flat on the ground plane, so it has NO INK CONTOUR LINES ANYWHERE. Every boundary in it is a change of flat colour, not a drawn line.

LAYOUT IS A TECHNICAL REQUIREMENT, because it sets the on-screen period of a scrolling layer: the upper THREE QUARTERS of the canvas are pure flat chroma magenta RGB 255,0,255 and hold no artwork whatsoever. The band lives entirely in the LOWEST QUARTER. Its body is fully opaque across the ENTIRE width with no hole, gap or magenta anywhere inside it, and it has two irregular boundaries, one along its top and one along its bottom, with flat magenta immediately above and immediately below.

Read from the top down, the band is: a lace of clean off-white sea foam, then a strip of dark saturated wet sand, then lighter damp sand as it dries.

The TOP boundary is the foam. It is the most varied edge in the image: rounded tongues of swash push up and thin fingers reach further than their neighbours, with shallow scallops between them, changing constantly along the width and never holding the same height for more than a short stretch. A few small detached rounded foam blobs may sit just above the main lace, still well inside the lowest quarter. The foam is a single flat off-white shape with a faint cool blue-grey underside, no outline, no bubbles, no spray, no droplets, no texture.

The BOTTOM boundary is where the wet sand dries. It is a restless scalloped edge of soft rounded lobes and shallow bays, obviously irregular, never a straight rule and never parallel to the foam above it. The colour steps from damp warm brown-gold to nothing at that edge, hard and clean against the magenta because a later layer of dry sand is composited underneath.

Colours are a tropical shore at midday: off-white foam, a dark cool amber-brown wet sand with a faint teal sheen where the water is thinnest, warming and lightening to a muted golden damp sand at the bottom. Broad flat areas of colour with at most one intermediate tone between wet and damp. Low contrast between the two sand values and strong contrast only at the foam.

Light is flat across the width: no directional sun, no sun glitter, no lateral gradient, brighter middle, vignette or side shadow. Vertical value change is wanted. Every artwork boundary is a HARD CEL EDGE against the perfectly flat magenta field, positively crisp and fully opaque, with no feathering, glow, blur, haze, transparency or magenta mixed into the artwork. No pink, mauve, purple or magenta anywhere in the artwork.

Seamlessness is ONLY the two outer edges continuing each other: at the left-most 4% and right-most 4% the band has exactly the same top foam height, the same bottom edge height, the same colours and the same values, quiet and interchangeable, with no distinctive tongue, lobe or foam blob crossing either boundary. Never achieve the wrap by repeating the interior: no arrangement, silhouette or colour sequence occurs twice, and there is no even rhythm, row or grid of foam tongues.

No sea, water body, horizon, sky, islands, boats, rocks, shells, driftwood, seaweed, footprints, characters, animals, birds, dunes, palms, ink outlines, cast shadows, text, letters, numerals, symbols, logos, frame or UI. No photographic texture, 3D rendering, fine repeated foam detail, grain or painterly brushwork.
