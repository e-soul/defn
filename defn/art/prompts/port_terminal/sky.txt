Create the sky layer of a modular tiling background set for DEFN, a contemporary-to-near-future military
belt-scroller. This image is not a scene. It is the backmost layer of a parallax stack: nothing but sky and
atmosphere, filling the frame edge to edge and top to bottom, with every other element of the port drawn on
separate layers in front of it. It contains no ground, no horizon line, no structures, and no silhouettes of
any kind.

No reference image is attached, deliberately. This layer shares no subject matter with any other DEFN asset,
and an attached background is reproduced rather than merely referenced: a first attempt carrying the port
apron as a palette reference drew the apron, lane markings and all, across the bottom third of the sky.

Draw it as authored 2D digital cartoon illustration in the DEFN house style. Sky is the one place the style
permits soft gradients, so use them: this layer is a smooth vertical wash and nothing else. It carries no
clouds at all -- the clouds are a separate drifting layer in front of it, and a cloud drawn here would sit
still while those move.

Grade the wash vertically. The top of the frame is a clean, slightly cool pale blue; it warms and lightens
steadily downward through a pale grey-blue into a soft warm cream haze band across the bottom third, the kind
of thickened light that sits over a working harbour on a bright overcast afternoon. The lowest fifth of the
frame is the palest and least saturated part of the image, because distant structures will be drawn against
it and need to separate from it.

Light the sky evenly across its width. There is no sun disc, no sun position, no bright patch, no glow, no god
rays, and no lateral brightness gradient of any kind: the left edge, the centre and the right edge are equally
bright. The only change in value across this image runs from top to bottom.

Make the image perfectly seamless from left to right for continuous horizontal scrolling. The value bands,
haze height, cloud distribution and every shape crossing one boundary must continue naturally at the opposite
boundary. With no clouds in it this is nearly automatic, but the wash must still not drift in brightness from
one side to the other.

Deliver only sky. The image is sky from the very top edge to the very bottom edge, with atmosphere as its only
subject. There is no ground, no concrete, no apron, no paving, no road, no lane markings, no sea, no water, no
land, no horizon line, no structures, no cranes, no ships, no masts, no birds, no aircraft, no people, no
clouds of any kind, and no lettering. The bottom edge of the frame is open haze that other layers will be drawn over.

Avoid photorealism, photographic cloud texture, realistic atmospheric scattering, volumetric light, painterly
concept-art brushwork, dramatic skies, storm clouds, sunsets, sun discs, lens flare, bloom, film grain, noisy
gradients, banding, clouds, a visible horizon, and visible tiling seams.
