One isolated shoreline strip for DEFN, a hand-authored 2D cartoon military belt-scroller. Broad flat local-colour shapes, restrained values, gently irregular hand-drawn edges. This is a cut-out layer over a chroma backdrop, not a landscape, and it is a surface lying flat on the ground plane, so it carries NO INK LINES OF ANY KIND. Every boundary in it is one flat colour meeting another.

WHAT THIS IS. Spent foam sliding up a flat beach, seen from high above at a shallow angle, looking down onto the sand. The wave broke somewhere off the top of the frame and what is left is a thin film of white foam washing up the sand and soaking away. The foam has NO THICKNESS: it is a flat stain of white lying on the beach, the way spilled milk lies on a table.

WHAT THIS IS NOT, and this is the whole brief. It is not a breaking wave. There is no crest, no curl, no barrel, no lip, no rolling edge, no wall of water, no whitecap, no spray, no droplets, no splash, no plume, no scalloped ridge of piled-up foam, and nothing with volume, roundness or a lit top and a shaded underside. Any shape that looks like a wave caught mid-break is a failed image. Nothing in this picture is three-dimensional and nothing is frozen in motion.

LAYOUT, BECAUSE IT IS ARITHMETIC. The canvas is an extremely wide, short strip. Measuring down from the top: the upper NINE TENTHS are pure flat chroma magenta RGB 255,0,255 with absolutely no artwork. All artwork is squeezed into the LAST TENTH of the height, along the bottom edge. The game scales this layer to a small fraction of the screen, so a shallow drawn band becomes a long on-screen tile and a deep one repeats every few seconds. A band that fills half the canvas is a failed image however good it looks.

Reading down that shallow strip:

* Its TOP boundary is where the foam meets open water, off the top of the frame. It is an irregular, roughly level edge that wanders gently along the width — a boundary between two flat colours, NOT a raised wave edge. No shape rises up from it.
* Below that, the foam: a broad flat field of clean off-white, thinning as it runs down the picture. It is broken and lacy, not solid. Ragged holes and gaps of wet sand show through it, more and more towards the bottom, and its lower boundary dissolves into fine irregular fingers, thin scallops and a scatter of small detached patches and lacy threads left stranded on the sand. Some fingers run much further down than their neighbours. The whole edge changes constantly along the width and never holds the same height for long.
* Below the foam, wet sand: a dark cool amber-brown just under the foam, with a faint teal sheen where the water is thinnest, lightening and warming steadily downward as it dries.

THE SAND COLOURS ARE EXACT AND MATTER. The wet sand immediately under the foam is about RGB 150,124,92, a warm mid brown. It then lightens and warms steadily downward, and along the very bottom row of the canvas it has reached exactly RGB 202,167,129, a soft warm golden sand. That final colour is fixed: this strip is composited onto a beach painted in that colour and the bottom row has to match it so the join disappears. The change from wet to dry is a smooth continuous vertical fade with no band, step, line or edge in it. Nothing here is chocolate, mud, olive, grey or green.

The foam is a single flat off-white fill with at most one faint cool grey tone in it. No interior modelling, no shading, no bubbles, no texture, no gradient inside the white, and NO OUTLINE. There is no dark line, dark rim, drop shadow, edge stroke or outline anywhere in this image, and especially not where the artwork meets the magenta. The colour simply stops. An outlined edge here would show as a drawn rule across the game screen.

Light is flat across the width: no directional sun, sun glitter, lateral gradient, brighter middle, vignette or side shadow. Vertical value change is wanted and is the whole point. Every artwork boundary is a HARD CEL EDGE against the perfectly flat magenta field, crisp and fully opaque, with no feathering, glow, blur, haze, transparency or magenta mixed into the artwork. No pink, mauve, purple or magenta anywhere in the artwork.

Seamlessness is ONLY the two outer edges continuing each other: at the left-most 4% and right-most 4% the foam field sits at the same height with the same quiet profile, the same colours and the same values, interchangeable, and no distinctive finger, hole or stranded patch crosses either boundary. Never achieve the wrap by repeating the interior: no arrangement or silhouette occurs twice, and there is no even rhythm, row or grid.

No sea, open water, waves, horizon, sky, islands, dry dunes, cliffs, rocks, shells, driftwood, seaweed, footprints, characters, animals, birds, palms, boats, cast shadows, text, letters, numerals, symbols, logos, frame or UI. No photographic texture, 3D rendering, fine repeated foam detail, grain or painterly brushwork.
