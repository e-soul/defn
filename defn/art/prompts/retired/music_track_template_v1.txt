## Mood

The music should feel:

- tactical;
- focused;
- energetic;
- slightly tense;
- mechanically precise;
- confident but not heroic;
- playful enough for a compact indie game;
- suitable for repeated listening.

It should suggest a battle that is constantly moving and evolving, where the player is observing, planning, and reacting.

## Avoid

Do not use vocals, spoken words, chanting, or lyrics.

Avoid:

- bombastic Hollywood trailer music;
- overly heroic military themes;
- patriotic or nationalistic associations;
- bleak war-drama emotion;
- horror;
- generic orchestral battle music;
- excessively aggressive metal;
- dominant dubstep drops;
- constant percussion with no dynamic variation;
- melodies that are too busy or attention-demanding;
- a strong final cadence if the track is intended to loop.

The result should sound like polished game background music, not a standalone pop song, film trailer, or military march.
