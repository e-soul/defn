Create one near band of beach debris along a high-tide line for DEFN, a contemporary military belt-scroller drawn as authored 2D cartoon illustration: clean dark hand-inked contours, crisp graphic silhouettes, simplified chunky geometry, broad flat local-colour regions, restrained two- or three-value cel shading, sparse purposeful texture and gently irregular hand-drawn edges. This is a cut-out layer over a chroma backdrop, not a complete landscape.

THE SHAPE OF THE DRAWN AREA IS THE HARDEST PART OF THIS BRIEF, SO IT COMES FIRST. The canvas is about 8000 pixels wide and 2048 pixels tall. The drawn debris must fit inside a strip about 8000 pixels wide and only about 550 pixels tall, sitting on the bottom edge of the canvas. Everything above that strip — roughly the top 1500 pixels, three quarters of the height — is pure flat chroma magenta RGB 255,0,255 and holds nothing at all. Not one twig, blade or pebble goes above the strip. The game squeezes this layer into a thin ribbon of screen, so a tall drawing repeats every couple of seconds and ruins the level; a wide, low, ribbon-shaped drawing is the whole point. If the picture looks like a tall pile, it is wrong however well it is drawn.

THE TWO SIDE MARGINS MATTER JUST AS MUCH. The left-most 5% and the right-most 5% of the width are COMPLETELY EMPTY magenta, top to bottom, with not one pebble, blade, twig or shell in them. The run of debris begins after the left margin and ends before the right margin, tapering away to nothing at each end; it never reaches, touches or is cut by either side edge. Those two bare margins are the entire wrapping mechanism: when the strip repeats, they meet as one wide stretch of open beach, which is exactly what is wanted.

Between the margins, a low continuous wrack line runs without a break: a shallow, uneven, opaque ridge of dried olive-brown seaweed, coarse shell grit and small dark pebbles, filling roughly the bottom third of the 550-pixel strip. Everything else rests on that ridge or on the ground beside it, so NOTHING can float: no object anywhere has empty magenta underneath it.

Lying in and on that ridge, spread evenly along the whole run so that no part of the width is emptier or busier than another: bleached grey driftwood logs and broken planks LYING FLAT or leaning at shallow angles, squat dark wet-looking rocks in small clusters, low coarse clumps of olive-green dune grass, coconut husks and scattered shells, and at most twice in the whole image a low weathered pale concrete block half-sunk in the sand. Nothing stands upright. There are no posts, stumps, stakes, trees or vertical timbers, and no single object rises more than about 400 pixels above the sand.

The TOP boundary is strongly varied, with deep frequent dips right down to the bare ridge between objects, because the sea behind this layer is seen only through those dips. Long open stretches of nothing but the low ridge are wanted and are not a failure.

The BOTTOM boundary is made of the objects' own undersides and the ridge's own scalloped silhouette, wandering restlessly in height with no straight stretch, with flat magenta immediately below it, rising a little between neighbouring objects. Keep the ink contour along that lower edge; it is what makes a log read as lying on the sand. NO shared sand apron, skirt, pale pad, platform, shelf, plinth, mound or coloured fill under the objects, and no contact shadow: that is added later by the build.

Colours: sun-bleached silver-grey driftwood with warm tan cores at the breaks, dark cool grey-brown rocks with a faint teal shadow, dried olive and khaki-brown seaweed, muted sage-olive grass, warm charcoal contours tinted to the scene rather than pure black. Outer silhouettes and major overlaps carry two to three times the weight of interior marks, with a few large readable splits and knots in the wood rather than fine grain everywhere.

Light is flat across the width, not lit from one side: no lateral gradient, sunbeams, side shadow, bright centre or vignette. Every artwork boundary is a HARD CEL EDGE against the perfectly flat magenta field, positively crisp and fully opaque, with no feathering, glow, blur, haze, transparency or magenta mixed into the artwork. No pink, mauve, purple or magenta anywhere in the artwork.

Never repeat the interior: no arrangement or silhouette occurs twice, never place an object directly above, below or behind another that resembles it, and do not organise the debris into rows, tiers, a grid or an even rhythm. Some objects lie alone with open ridge either side, some cluster tight, and the gaps are of obviously different widths. Do not draw two separate wrack lines stacked one above the other; there is exactly one.
